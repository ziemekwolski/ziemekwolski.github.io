<section class="coaching-section coaching-story">
  <div class="coaching-wrap coaching-narrow" markdown="1">

You’re already doing strong technical work, but hard work alone doesn’t make promotion inevitable. When expectations are vague, you can spend years taking on more projects without building the evidence, visibility, and sponsorship that promotion committees need to see.

<p class="coaching-callout">As an Engineering Manager, I helped roughly 10 engineers earn promotions—from Junior through to Staff.</p>

Through focused coaching and clear feedback, I helped them prove their impact, build confidence, and move forward. That was the best part of my job.

This three-month coaching program brings that experience directly to you. Together, we’ll turn your work into a clear Staff Engineer case, so you know what to focus on, demonstrate leadership at the right level, and enter promotion conversations with confidence.
