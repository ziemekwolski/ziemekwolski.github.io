# The hardest part, hitting it again when your hand already hurts

The hardest part, **hitting it again when your hand already hurts!** So, what does this have to do with work?

Last week I was at my kid's Taekwondo grading.

One of the events is breaking boards.

It’s simple; hit a board hard enough and it breaks.

So you try.<br>
Hit once and it doesn’t break.<br>
Hit again, it doesn’t break.

At this point your hand is already hurting. You start to question your capability.

Eventually, you hit the board and it breaks!

**That’s your breakthrough moment!**

You realize that all the while you were making progress and that you are capable of overcoming hard things.

Similarly at work.

You face challenges.

You do difficult things; you try to make progress.<br>
It might not feel like you are making progress, but …

**Your breakthrough might be one more attempt away!**
