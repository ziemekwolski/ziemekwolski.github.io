Who it's for

You're ready to grow. You need a clearer way forward.

For experienced software engineers navigating the challenges that come with growing responsibility.

Your goals might involve a promotion, more technical leadership, or simply a better way to navigate your work. We'll start with what matters to you.

This may be a fit if you want to…

  • Feel more confident in your judgment and contributions
  • Get clearer on priorities and handle a demanding workload
  • Navigate feedback, manager relationships, or difficult conversations
  • Grow into technical leadership while staying close to engineering
  • Prepare for a promotion or decide what you want your next step to be

Or you're in a situation like this…

  • Your role is growing faster than your support around it
  • There's a lot of change at work (new tools like AI, reorgs, shifting expectations) and you want to stay valuable through it
  • You want stronger reviews and clearer conversations with your manager
  • You're not sure of your next step

The outcome

Progress that matters to you.

The goal isn't a particular title at any cost. It's to help you approach your work and career with greater clarity, confidence, and intention.

If advancement is your goal, we can work on understanding expectations, gathering evidence of impact, and preparing for review conversations. Your employer controls promotion decisions and timing.

Depending on your goals, we might work on…

  • A clearer set of priorities
  • A plan for a difficult conversation
  • Ways to communicate your work and decisions
  • Practical habits for focus and reflection
  • A strategy for career growth or promotion
