My background

I understand the work and the challenges around it.

I'm Ziemek, a former Engineering Manager and a coach for experienced engineers.

I've seen how much career growth depends on things that aren't always taught in engineering: making decisions with incomplete information, communicating across teams, navigating feedback, and finding the confidence to step up.

My coaching combines an understanding of engineering work with thoughtful questions and practical tools. We'll focus on your situation not force you into someone else's definition of success.

Sometimes that means preparing for a promotion. Sometimes it means finding a better way to manage your workload, lead a conversation, or trust your own judgment again.
