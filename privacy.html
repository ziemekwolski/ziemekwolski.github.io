Last updated: November 15, 2026

I'm Ziemek Wolski, a coach for software engineers based in Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what personal information I collect through this website and through coaching, why I collect it, and the choices you have. I handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What I collect

  • When you book a call. Bookings go through Calendly. I receive your name, email address, and anything you choose to share in the booking questions.
  • When you email me. Your email address and whatever you include in your message.
  • When we work together. Notes and records I keep about our coaching sessions ("coaching records"), and the contact and billing details needed to run the engagement.
  • When you comment on a post. Comments are handled by Disqus. What you post is public, and Disqus collects its own information about you (see below).
  • When you visit the site. This site has no analytics and no advertising trackers of its own. The services that host and power it can see technical information such as your IP address and browser type (see below).

How I use it

  • To schedule and hold calls, and to reply to your messages.
  • To provide coaching, prepare for sessions, and follow up on what we discussed.
  • To manage our agreement, invoicing, and records I'm required to keep.

I don't sell your personal information, and I don't use it for advertising. I won't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Confidentiality in coaching

What you share in coaching stays confidential. I won't share information that identifies you with anyone without your written consent, except in the limited situations below, which are also set out in our coaching agreement:

  • I reasonably believe you pose an imminent risk of harm to yourself or others.
  • I suspect abuse, neglect, or exploitation of a child, an elder, or a vulnerable person.
  • Disclosure is required by law, a court order, or a government authority.
  • I consult a supervisor, mentor, or professional advisor to improve my coaching. When I do, I limit identifying details as much as reasonably possible.

Coaching is not therapy or legal advice, and its confidentiality may not be protected to the same extent as conversations with a licensed therapist or lawyer.

Coaching records

I may keep notes and other records about our work together. I protect them with reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, and I keep them only as long as I need them for coaching and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

Email and messaging apps aren't fully secure. If you choose to share sensitive details that way, please keep that in mind.

Services I use

These providers process information on my behalf, or when you use their features on this site. Some of them store data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law.

  • Calendly runs the booking calendar and sets cookies on the booking page. It asks for your consent to optional cookies, and booking works either way. For your booking details, I decide how they're used and Calendly processes them for me. Calendly privacy notice.
  • Disqus provides comments on blog posts. It only loads when you choose "Show comments". Once loaded, it uses cookies and may track you across other sites that use Disqus. You can control its data sharing in your Disqus settings. Disqus privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts on this site, so your browser connects to Google's servers. Google privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or withdraw your consent to my using it (subject to legal or contractual limits). You can also ask me to delete it where I'm not required to keep it. I'll respond within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with my response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
